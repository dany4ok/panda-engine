Date: 2026-11-24
Hardware: RTX 5070 Ti 16 GB (14.9 GiB free at test start), 23.3 GiB free RAM

         two Poly-strata server configs. NOT changed for this run.

WHY THE THREE INKLING QUANTS FAIL
---------------------------------
Not corrupt artifacts: weights load successfully (Q3_K_XL 13.0 GiB in 4.5 s,
IQ4_XS 15.0 GiB in 5.9 s, APEX 12.4 GiB in 5.1 s). Startup then aborts in
"initializing runtime":

  Inkling 2: the KV caches, histories, checkpoints and drafter state of 1
  sequence(s) of 131072 tokens need 2530 MiB beside 512 MiB for the workspace
  and CUDA graphs, and 1115/0/1537 MiB are free after the weights; lower
  --max-concurrency or --max-context

Free VRAM after weights: Q3_K_XL 1115 MiB, IQ4_XS 0 MiB, APEX 1537 MiB.
Required: 2530 MiB KV + 512 MiB workspace = 3042 MiB. Context was deliberately
NOT lowered for this benchmark. Log files (panda engine\logs):
  final-20261124-163647-inkling-q3kxl.err.log
  final-20261124-163647-inkling-iq4xs.err.log
  final-20261124-163647-inkling-apex.err.log

A separate exploratory run (not part of these results, context changed) showed
these three quants do answer with host expert residency at 131072 context:
  iq4xs host: gen 54.3 t/s, read 656.9 t/s
  q3kxl host: gen 66.4 t/s, read 1090.7 t/s
  apex  host: gen 68.6 t/s, read 1317.5 t/s
They were removed from this PC afterwards; their artifacts can be re-downloaded
from the URLs in panda/catalog.json.
