This run supersedes the one-pass sweep: more probes, 4 independent runs per
variant, plus loop and 128k-context probes. No temperature override was sent;
engine defaults were used for all requests.

METHOD
------
1. Accuracy set: 23 fixed objective problems (arithmetic, classic traps,
   logic, sequences). Each answer graded automatically against a per-problem
   regex. Problems were split into 3 batches (8/8/7) with a fixed system
   prompt and the instruction 'one line per answer, format N) answer'.
   4 runs per variant = 92 graded answers per variant.
   max_tokens=16384 for every request.
2. Single-problem mode: the same 23 problems, one request per problem,
   max_tokens=8192 (used to check the effect of batching).
3. Loop / endurance probe: 'write numbers 1..200, one per line'.
4. Context probe: needle 'CODUL SECRET ESTE ZX7-QM42' inserted at ~50% depth
   of a 129652-token haystack; question at the end, max_tokens=1024.
5. Output-cap probe: 10 problems with max_tokens=6144 vs 16384.

`high` is penalised by one single event: in one run a 7-problem batch came
back empty (finish_reason=length) after consuming all 16384 tokens in
reasoning. In the other three runs it was perfect. It is not weak, it is
unstable on large batches.

The most frequently missed problem is '8 balls, one heavier: minimum
weighings on a balance' (correct answer: 2). low missed it 3 times, medium
once, high once, max once, xhigh never.

SPEED
-----
Decode throughput is flat across variants: ~140 tok/s. reasoning_effort
changes how many tokens are thought, not how fast they are generated.
Prompt processing (prefill): ~3.3-3.9k tok/s (84k tokens in 25.1 s,
42k in 11.6 s, 129k in 33.4 s).
Prompt caching works: the identical 129652-token prompt dropped from
~45-50 s cold to ~7.0 s with 129651 cached tokens.

LOOPS / RUNAWAY
---------------
Counting probe 1..200: all 5 variants returned 200/200 numbers in order,
zero duplicates. No infinite loops on repetitive tasks.
Some repetition in reasoning was observed (29 repeated 12-word shingles for
high, 10 for xhigh in one run) but did not degenerate.
The real risk is reasoning runaway: on a single prompt containing all 23
problems, xhigh hit the 16384-token cap with empty content in 2 out of 3
runs; high did the same once in 6 batches. With batches of <=8 problems,
xhigh had zero truncations.

OUTPUT-CAP FOOTGUN (confirmed)
------------------------------
With max_tokens=6144 on a 10-problem batch, both xhigh and max consumed all
6142 tokens in reasoning and emitted no final answer (finish_reason=length,
content=""). With max_tokens=16384 both answered; xhigh scored 10/10 and
max 9/10 (it called the person in the photo a daughter instead of a son).
Reasoning effort without output headroom produces an empty reply.

128k CONTEXT
------------
Engine limit is 131072 tokens (declared and enforced). A prompt of 12500
repetitions was rejected with context_length_exceeded.
129652 tokens accepted; the needle at ~50% depth was retrieved by all five
variants (5/5) in ~45-50 s cold, ~7 s with a warm prefix cache.
128k is functional, not merely declared.

RECOMMENDATIONS
---------------
1. Use `xhigh` when accuracy matters: 100% here, no truncation at batch <= 8.
2. Use `medium` for routine work: 97.8% at roughly half the tokens and time
   of xhigh.
3. `low` is fastest and cheapest (108 tok/answer) but misses logic traps.
4. Avoid `max`: slowest and most expensive with no accuracy gain.
5. Be careful with `high`: perfect in 3 of 4 runs, empty reply in the 4th.
6. Do not send 20+ problems in one prompt to high/xhigh. Keep batches <= 8
   and max_tokens >= 16384 so a high-effort answer can actually be emitted.

LIMITATIONS
-----------
Regex-based grading on 23 problems; a single engine build and flag set;
no temperature override; one machine. The high runaway and the xhigh
batching failure were each observed once/twice, so they are risks rather
than deterministic per-prompt behaviour.
