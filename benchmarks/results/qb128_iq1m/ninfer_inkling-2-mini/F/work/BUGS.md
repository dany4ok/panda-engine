# Simptome raportate

- `mean([1,2,3,4])` returneaza 1.5 in loc de 2.5.
- `median([3,1,2])` returneaza 1 in loc de 2.
- `percentile([1,2,3,4,5], 50)` returneaza 4 in loc de 3.
- `summarize(...)` returneaza total/avg gresite desi datele au cheia `score`.
- `main(['1','2','3'])` afiseaza 5.0 in loc de 6.0.
- `main(['--help'])` nu afiseaza usage si nu returneaza 0.
